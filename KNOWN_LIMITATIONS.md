# Known limitations

1. **Simulated mGEN ledger, no real GEN moves.** There is no native GEN escrow, payment, withdrawal, or real token staking. Each address can claim 10000 demo credits once. New addresses can obtain fresh credits; this is not Sybil-resistant monetary issuance.
2. This release targets Stable Studionet only: Chain ID 61999 and `https://studio.genlayer.com/api`. Studionet consensus finality is distinct from application state FINALIZED. Clients must wait for a successful FINALIZED transaction and then read `latest-final` state.
3. Anyone may finalize a pending verdict immediately. There is no timed challenge period or guaranteed opportunity to challenge; transaction ordering decides whether challenge or finalization occurs first.
4. The first verdict, second verdict and contradiction witnesses are append-only within the task. Validators independently fetch and score, but their intermediate answers are not separately persisted. Quality scoring remains subjective; consensus can be undetermined. No result is written when execution or consensus fails.
5. Delivery must be public HTTPS UTF-8 text, at most 24000 response bytes. Private resources, authenticated APIs, binary files and dynamic pages are unsupported. Hashes cover the exact body returned by `gl.nondet.web.get`, not rendered HTML. Mutable sources may change or disappear. A changed body produces hash rejection; an unreachable resource produces a retryable error.
6. A poster may explicitly expire an OPEN or CLAIMED task before submission, refunding the bounty without slashing. There is no automatic deadline. Stake is retained in the simulation after settlement; topping up is supported, withdrawal is not.
7. Three agents and three tasks marked `seed_demo` are read-only illustrative fixtures and excluded from issued-funds accounting and consensus evidence. They are not funded user balances.
8. Temporary test accounts exist in browser memory only and are lost on reload. Their simulated balances and authored records remain on-chain, but control of those accounts is lost. Use an injected wallet to retain an address. Never enter recovery phrases or private keys into this application.
9. The application has no contract upgrade, admin settlement override, deletion or appeal beyond one challenge. Reputation can be negative. An address may own one agent and shares its reputation when acting as poster.
10. Local mocks validate protocol branches; they are not real validator, wallet or mobile-device results. Actual chain evidence is limited to the transactions and read-backs recorded in `evidence/`.
