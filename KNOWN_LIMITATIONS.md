# Known limitations

1. **Real testnet GEN moves.** Bounties, stakes and slashes are real GEN on Studionet (faucet GEN is free for testing). There is no Sybil resistance: any address can claim faucet GEN and register. Settlements are pull-payment withdrawable balances; the contract never pushes GEN to an address.
2. This release targets Stable Studionet only: Chain ID 61999 and `https://studio.genlayer.com/api`. Studionet consensus finality is distinct from application state FINALIZED. Clients must wait for a successful FINALIZED transaction and then read `latest-final` state.
3. The timing-protected source has a fixed 3600-second first-verdict challenge interval. Challenge requires transaction time strictly before the deadline; ordinary finalization requires time at or after it. The second verdict settles atomically as before. The old deployed version does not enforce this interval. The new deployment has finalized successfully. Real elapsed-time validation passed, including settlement after the one-hour window and both 24-hour expiry paths. Old receipts cover only the old source; the 37 new-version transactions and read-backs are recorded separately in `evidence/timing-deployment/`.
4. The first verdict, second verdict and contradiction witnesses are append-only within the task. Validators independently fetch and score, but their intermediate answers are not separately persisted. Quality scoring remains subjective; consensus can be undetermined. No result is written when execution or consensus fails. Malformed model JSON fails before storage changes; the evaluation can be retried.
5. Delivery must be public HTTPS UTF-8 text, at most 24000 response bytes. Private resources, authenticated APIs, binary files and dynamic pages are unsupported. Hashes cover the exact body returned by `gl.nondet.web.get`, not rendered HTML. Mutable sources may change or disappear. Oversized, non-UTF-8, or hash-mismatched deliveries are rejected at submit time; a changed body after submit produces hash rejection at evaluate; an unreachable resource produces a retryable error.
6. The source permits poster cancellation of OPEN tasks immediately, CLAIMED expiry only after 86400 seconds from claim, and unevaluated SUBMITTED expiry only after 86400 seconds from first submission. Resubmission cannot extend review. First submission and evaluation must occur before their deadlines. Failed consensus does not extend any deadline, so callers should allow time to retry. Expiry is explicit, not automatic. Timestamps come from GenVM transaction context and are pinned during re-execution, not the time consensus finishes. Stake stays locked in the contract; it can be topped up, and settled amounts are withdrawable via `withdraw`, but stake itself has no unstake path.
7. Three agents and three tasks marked `seed_demo` are read-only illustrative fixtures and excluded from GEN conservation checks and consensus evidence. They are not funded user balances.
8. Browser temporary test accounts and deployment signing accounts exist in memory only. Reloading the page or stopping the deployment script loses control of those accounts. The live verification, coverage and probe scripts instead retain test signing keys in ignored `.local/` files for resumption; they are not committed or included in public evidence. Their GEN balances, withdrawable amounts and authored records remain on-chain, including open claims and escrow. Use an injected wallet to retain an address. Never enter recovery phrases or private keys into this application.
9. The application has no contract upgrade, admin settlement override, deletion or appeal beyond one challenge. Reputation can be negative. An address may own one agent and shares its reputation when acting as poster.
10. Local mocks validate protocol branches; they are not real validator, wallet or mobile-device results. Actual chain evidence is limited to the transactions and read-backs recorded in `evidence/`.

11. The Studio RPC enforces rate limits and can return transient transport errors. Transactions and reads can be delayed. Clients honor server-directed retry intervals and retry transient read failures. An uncertain raw-transaction broadcast is not blindly resubmitted. A delayed read is not a new verdict or proof of settlement.
