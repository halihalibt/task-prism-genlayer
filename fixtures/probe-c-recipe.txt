Chocolate cake recipe

Ingredients: 200 g plain flour, 200 g caster sugar, 75 g cocoa powder,
2 teaspoons baking powder, 1 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda, 2 large eggs,
250 ml whole milk, 125 ml vegetable oil, 2 teaspoons vanilla extract,
250 ml just-boiled water, a pinch of salt.

Method: Heat the oven to 180 C and line two 20 cm sandwich tins. Whisk the
dry ingredients together in a large bowl. Beat in the eggs, milk, oil and
vanilla until smooth. Stir in the boiled water; the batter will be thin.
Divide between the tins and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until a skewer comes
out clean. Cool in the tins for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.
Sandwich with chocolate buttercream and dust the top with cocoa.
